title: Ratings, comments, reports
description: Rate series, join discussions, and report issues on the server your reader app is connected to.

Use the reader app to share ratings, discuss series and chapters, and report catalogue errors on the server it is connected to. This guide covers how to rate titles, participate in discussions, format comments, and report problems to moderators.

## Rate a series

Every series in the connected server’s catalogue can receive a community rating on a scale from 1 to 10 stars.

### Two ratings

A series can show two ratings, and each has its own mark:

- **The yellow star** is the average score from readers on the connected server. This is the rating you add to when you rate a series.
- **The orange star** is the rating from MangaDex. It appears only when MangaDex has a rating for the series.

A cover shows the yellow star first and the orange star under it. A series with one rating shows that one only. A card says `Unrated` when there is no rating to show.

You can hide the MangaDex rating in [Settings](/guide/accounts#mangadex-ratings).

### Submit a rating

To rate a series, open its series page:

1. Click the star button in the action row beneath the title.
2. In the rating popover, move your cursor or slide your finger across the row of ten stars to choose your score.
3. Click or release your finger to submit the rating.

Your rating saves immediately to your account on that server. The average score and total number of community votes displayed on the series page refresh to reflect the updated aggregate.

### Remove a rating

To withdraw your rating, open the rating popover on the series page and click Remove.

The Remove button appears only when you have not submitted a written review body. Removing your score deletes your rating record from the database.

Submitting and modifying ratings requires you to be signed in to an account on that server. For help with signing in, see [Accounts](/guide/accounts).

## Comments and discussions

Komiq provides unified comment threads on series pages and at the conclusion of every chapter.

### Where to discuss

- Series discussion: Open any series page and select the Discussion tab. This area hosts general conversations about the series, story arcs, and recommendations.
- Chapter discussion: In the reader, scroll past the final page of any chapter to reach the comment section. This area is intended for reactions and analysis specific to that chapter.

### Write a comment

To post a comment, you must be signed in to your account on the connected server. Enter your text into the composer area.

- Spoiler warnings: Check the Contains spoilers box if your comment mentions plot twists, character deaths, or future developments. Comments flagged as spoilers appear covered by a Spoiler veil that readers can tap to reveal your message.
- Attach an image: Click the image icon to upload an image from your device. A thumbnail preview appears above the composer controls. Click the remove icon on the preview if you decide not to include the image.
- Submit: Click Post comment to publish your message.

### Reply to comments

To reply to an existing comment, click Reply below the comment text. An inline composer opens beneath the message.

Replies display as a threaded discussion tree. To keep conversations readable on mobile devices, threads indent visually up to four levels deep while preserving true reply relationships.

### Vote on comments

Readers signed in on the connected server can vote on other readers’ comments there:

- Click the thumbs up icon to like a comment.
- Click the thumbs down icon to dislike a comment.
- Click an active vote a second time to clear it.

### Draft protection

If you enter text into a comment composer or attach an image without posting, komiq tracks the unsaved draft. If you attempt to leave the page or close the tab, the application displays a confirmation prompt asking whether you want to discard your draft.

## Report a problem

If you find an error in the catalogue, broken images, or abusive comments, you can send a report to the moderation queue.

### How reports are handled

All reports are sent to the connected server’s administrative queue for human review.

The report system sends no status emails and provides no support inbox. When you submit a report, the screen displays a unique reference code. Keep this reference code if you report the same issue again. You do not need an account to submit a report.

### Report topics

You can file reports from `/support` or `/support/report`. The form categorises issues into five topics:

1. Two series merged into one: Use this topic when a single catalogue entry combines chapters from two distinct stories. Provide the link to the entry and identify which chapters belong elsewhere.
2. The same series listed twice: Use this topic when duplicate entries exist for one work under different titles. Provide links to both entries if available.
3. A series is missing: Use this topic to report a work that does not exist in the catalogue. Provide the exact title. An author name or an external link also helps identify the work.
4. Someone in the comments: Use this topic to report harassment, offensive language, spam, or malicious spoilers. If you click Report directly on a comment, komiq automatically attaches the comment identifier. When you submit the report, the server saves an excerpt of the comment body and looks up its author. If you report through the general form, provide the author username.
5. Something else: Use this topic for incorrect covers, metadata errors, chapters that fail to open, or other unlisted issues.

### Report from the reader

When reading a chapter, you can report problems without opening the support page manually. Open the reader menu, scroll to the bottom, and click Report a problem. This opens the report form with the series title and chapter number automatically filled in. For more details on reader tools, see [Reading](/guide/reading).

# Komiq Copyright Policy

**Effective:** August 9, 2026  
**Applies to:** Komiq-operated services, including the work-in-progress demo at
`komiq.cc`

This policy is an operational statement, not legal advice or a representation that
Komiq qualifies for a limitation of liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512 or any other
law. Self-hosted deployments are operated independently and must maintain their own
copyright processes.

## Role of Komiq

Komiq develops reader software and operates community-configured intermediary
infrastructure. Eligible users can add third-party sources, which then become
available globally. When a user requests a cover or page, Komiq may retrieve it from
a source and, on the web path, temporarily cache the response at Cloudflare's edge.
Komiq is not intended to operate a permanent central archive of manga or comic image
files, but Cloudflare, Suwayomi, browser, app, or device caches and user-enabled
downloads may hold copies for varying periods.

Komiq does not claim ownership of third-party works and does not grant a license to
them. Source availability does not mean that Komiq endorses a source or has determined
that every item it exposes is authorized.

## User and source requirements

You may add or use only sources and material that you are legally authorized to
access. You must comply with applicable law, copyright restrictions, and source terms.
You may not use Komiq to infringe copyright, evade access controls, misrepresent a
source's authorization, or interfere with a source's services.

Trusted-source privileges are conditional. Komiq may restrict or revoke them, suspend
accounts, disable access to particular locations, or remove a source when reasonably
necessary to address infringement, abuse, security risk, or legal obligations.

## Copyright notices

Only a copyright owner or a person authorized to act for the owner should submit a
copyright notice. A notice should include:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the owner or authorized agent.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list if the notice
   covers multiple works.
3. Identification of the material or activity at issue and information reasonably
   sufficient for Komiq to locate it. Include each relevant Komiq URL, source name,
   title, chapter, and upstream URL when available.
4. Your name, organization, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the complained-of use is not
   authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you
   are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

### Notice contact — WIP and public-launch blocker

Komiq has not yet published a dedicated copyright-notice channel or registered a
designated DMCA agent. Do not treat a repository issue, pull request, social-media
message, or contributor's personal address as a formal notice channel. Complete agent
contact information must be published here before the operated demo is treated as a
public production service.

## How Komiq handles reports

Komiq's policy is to review sufficiently specific, good-faith reports promptly and
take proportionate action within its available technical capabilities. Depending on
the circumstances, that may include disabling a Komiq URL, suspending or removing a
source, revoking trusted-source privileges, restricting an account, or requesting
additional information.

Komiq may notify an affected user or source submitter and may preserve the notice,
related communications, and enforcement record for legal compliance, security,
appeals, and repeat-infringer enforcement. Komiq may forward a notice, including the
claimant's submitted contact information, when reasonably necessary to process it or
as required by law.

Submitting a report does not guarantee removal. Komiq may reject notices that are
incomplete, concern material it cannot locate or control, or appear fraudulent or
materially misleading. Nothing in this policy limits Komiq's ability to remove or
restrict material under its terms or for other lawful reasons.

## Counter-notices

An affected user who believes access was restricted because of mistake or
misidentification may submit a counter-notice after the dedicated contact channel is
published. It should include:

1. The user's physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material and its location before access was disabled.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the user has a good-faith belief the
   restriction resulted from mistake or misidentification.
4. The user's name, address, and telephone number, plus consent to the jurisdiction
   required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3) and acceptance of service of process from the
   original claimant or its agent.

Komiq may forward a counter-notice to the original claimant. Access will be restored
only when appropriate under applicable law, the service's rules, and the facts of the
case. This policy does not promise restoration.

## Repeat-infringer policy

In appropriate circumstances, Komiq will terminate accounts or revoke source-adding
privileges of users who repeatedly infringe or repeatedly submit infringing sources.
Komiq may consider the number, timing, severity, apparent validity, and disposition of
reports; counter-notices; court orders; retractions; and evidence of deliberate abuse.
A fixed notice count is not promised because duplicate, mistaken, fraudulent, or
withdrawn notices may require different treatment.

Users may not evade enforcement by creating another account. A source may be disabled
globally when repeated violations show that narrower measures are ineffective.

## Misrepresentation and abuse

Do not knowingly make false claims or materially misrepresent that material is
infringing or was removed by mistake. Komiq may reject abusive submissions, restrict
people who misuse the reporting process, and refer suspected fraud or threats to the
appropriate authorities. United States law may impose liability for certain material
misrepresentations in notices or counter-notices.
